5. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
- A very small p-value is not necessarily a practically important effect, especially with a large dataset.
- The 1–5 score is ordinal; a t-test on the mean treats rating steps as approximately equally spaced.
- Statistical significance does not establish causation.
- Review volume reflects this dataset's collection process and should not automatically be treated as all Amazon reviews.
- Timestamps are converted to UTC; local dates may differ.
- Repeated review keys and unusual values are flagged for investigation rather than automatically deleted.
- Text length does not measure sentiment; sentiment analysis would be a separate analysis.
